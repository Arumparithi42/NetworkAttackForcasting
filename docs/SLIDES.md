# SIH26153 – 5-slide technical presentation (+ 2-minute demo script)

Figures referenced below are generated by `scripts/evaluate.py` into `reports/`.

---

## Slide 1 – Problem

**Detection after the fact is too late.**

- Classic IDS: *"is this flow malicious?"* → fires once the attack is already visible.
- An infiltration is a **process over time**: a compromised host probes internal servers, then
  access attempts follow.
- SIH26153 asks for a **world model**: learn P(S<sub>t+1</sub> | S<sub>≤t</sub>) of the network,
  simulate K windows ahead, estimate the probability of an attack trajectory, map it to MITRE
  ATT&CK and explain it – offline, for enterprise and Critical Information Infrastructure.

Visual: timeline "benign → probing → access attempts" with *detection* marked at the attack and
*forecast* marked before it.

---

## Slide 2 – Proposed solution

**Host-centric recurrent world model + calibrated forecasting + evidence you can audit.**

- State S<sub>t</sub> per host per minute: 67 flow + packet + graph + temporal features (TTL
  variance, retransmissions, new peers, SYN-without-reply, service mix …).
- GRU world model: Gaussian next-state head (dynamics) + stage head applied to **imagined** future
  states; 32 Monte-Carlo futures → P(attack within 5 min) with uncertainty band; calibrated.
- Integrated-Gradients explanations rendered as sentences with the real traffic values.
- Observed / Inferred / Predicted separation; ATT&CK v19.2 tactics + candidate techniques.
- Tamper-evident forecast ledger (hash chain + Ed25519, optional EVM anchor) – the blockchain
  part is used for audit integrity, not forced into the model.

## Slide 3 – Architecture

(Diagram from `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` §2 + dashboard screenshot.)

- Real data: CSE-CIC-IDS2018 – 8 usable days of flow CSVs (network level) + **116 GB of raw
  per-host PCAPs streamed** for the two infiltration days (host level, packet features).
- 9 data defects found and fixed (12-hour clock, UTC−4, 67,973 contradictory duplicate labels, …).
- Leakage control: chronological per-family split, purge gaps, causal features (unit-tested),
  scaler/calibrator/threshold fitted on train/validation only.
- Offline stack: Python, PyTorch (CPU), scikit-learn, Captum, dpkt, Streamlit, SQLite.

## Slide 5 – Impact & future scope

- **What works today:** an offline, explainable, calibrated forecasting pipeline on real traffic
  with an audit trail; continuation/stage forecasting of ongoing attacks; honest evaluation that
  separates detection from genuine early warning.
- **What the evidence says:** onset forecasting needs data with real precursors (multi-stage
  campaigns). Next: train on multi-stage datasets (DARPA 2000 LLDOS, LANL red-team auth logs,
  CTU-13 botnet life-cycles) and on an organisation's own history.
- **Deployment path for CII:** live NetFlow/IPFIX or PCAP ingestion, per-site recalibration on
  benign traffic, analyst feedback loop, ledger anchors shared with a sector CERT / NCIIPC
  (permissioned ledger, hashes only).
- Research extensions: temporal GNN for multi-hop lateral movement, mixture-density dynamics for
  multi-modal futures, counterfactual defence planning with the what-if simulator.
